Confidence Is Built

Confidence is not just pretending everything is fine. It comes from preparation, past success, good routines, and knowing you have handled hard moments before.

Ways To Build It

  1. Keep track of good outings and good bullpens.
  2. Remember specific moments when you competed well.
  3. Prepare the same way before games.
  4. Focus on actions instead of stats.

Small Wins Matter

A good pitch, a good reset, or attacking the zone after missing badly are all wins. Confidence grows when a pitcher notices those moments.
