Why Routine Matters

A pre-pitch routine helps a pitcher stop thinking about random stuff and focus on the next pitch. Without a routine, it is easy to spiral after a bad pitch or a walk.

Basic Routine

  1. Step behind the rubber.
  2. Take one deep breath.
  3. Look at the target.
  4. Say one short cue like “through it” or “compete.”
  5. Step on the rubber and throw with trust.

Keep It Short

The routine should not take forever. The goal is to reset quickly and make the next pitch with confidence.
