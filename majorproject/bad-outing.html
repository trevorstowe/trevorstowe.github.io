Bad Outings Happen

Every pitcher has bad outings. Sometimes you walk guys, miss spots, or feel like nothing is working. One bad inning does not define who you are as a player.

What To Do After

  1. Write down what happened honestly.
  2. Pick one thing you can improve.
  3. Do not replay the outing all night.
  4. Get back to work the next day.

Useful Question

Instead of asking, “Why am I terrible?” ask, “What is one thing I can take from this?” That question actually helps.
