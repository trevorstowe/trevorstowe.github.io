The Problem

Overthinking usually starts when a pitcher cares too much about the result. One ball turns into two balls, then the mind starts racing. Instead of competing, the pitcher starts aiming and trying not to mess up.

Simple Mindset Steps

  1. Accept that one bad pitch does not matter.
  2. Pick one simple target.
  3. Commit to the pitch fully.
  4. Move on right after the pitch is thrown.

What To Tell Yourself

A good cue is: “Win this pitch.” That is better than thinking about the whole inning, your stats, or what the coach is thinking.
