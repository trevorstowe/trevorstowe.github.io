Do Not Overload Yourself

Mechanics matter, but thinking about too many mechanical things during a game can make pitching harder. A pitcher should usually have one simple cue, not a full science project happening in his head.

Good In-Game Cues

  • Stay through the plate.
  • Sit in the back leg.
  • Finish toward the target.
  • Stay athletic.

Save Big Fixes For Practice

During a game, the goal is to compete. Big mechanical changes should usually happen during practice, bullpens, or catch play.
