Finding Confidence Again

A story about baseball, overthinking, and learning how to trust myself again.

The Start

I felt confident going into the game. Everything felt simple and under control.

The First Miss

I missed one pitch, and instead of moving on, I started thinking about everything.

The Spiral

The more I thought, the worse it got. I stopped trusting myself and tried to control everything.

The Reset

I stepped off, took a breath, and reminded myself to keep it simple.

The Comeback

I started attacking again, and slowly the confidence came back.
