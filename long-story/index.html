Finding Confidence Again

A short story about baseball, overthinking, and learning how to trust myself again.

The Start

Before the game, I felt ready. My arm felt good, my body felt loose, and I believed I could attack the strike zone.

The First Miss

Then I missed one pitch. Instead of moving on, I started thinking about my mechanics, my release point, and everything that could go wrong.

The Spiral

The more I thought, the harder pitching felt. I stopped trusting myself and started trying to control every little part of the throw.

The Reset

I finally stepped off, took a breath, and reminded myself to stay simple. See the target. Trust my body. Compete.

The Comeback

The confidence did not come back all at once, but it started to return when I stopped trying to be perfect and started attacking again.
