Chocolate Chip Cookies

Soft, simple, and always good

About

This recipe is basic in a good way. The cookies come out soft in the middle with slightly crispy edges and they are pretty hard to mess up.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 2 1/4 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F
  2. Mix dry ingredients
  3. Mix butter and sugar
  4. Add eggs and vanilla
  5. Combine everything
  6. Add chocolate chips
  7. Bake 9 to 11 minutes
