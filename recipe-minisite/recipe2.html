Pancakes

Easy breakfast that actually tastes good

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 3 tbsp butter

Directions

  1. Mix ingredients
  2. Pour onto pan
  3. Flip when bubbles form
  4. Cook until golden
