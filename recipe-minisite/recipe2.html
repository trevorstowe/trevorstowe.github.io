Pancakes

Pancakes are one of the easiest breakfast foods to make, and they are worth it when you want something better than cereal but not too complicated.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Stir everything until the batter is mostly smooth.
  4. Heat a pan or griddle over medium heat.
  5. Pour some batter onto the pan.
  6. Cook until bubbles show up on top, then flip it.
  7. Cook the other side until golden brown.
  8. Serve with syrup, butter, or whatever else you want on them.
