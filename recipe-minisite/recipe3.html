Grilled Cheese

This is probably the most simple recipe here, but it is still one of the best. Crispy bread and melted cheese is not exactly a complicated formula, but it works.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 slices of cheese
  • 1 tablespoon butter

Instructions

  1. Butter one side of each slice of bread.
  2. Put one slice of bread butter side down in a pan.
  3. Add the cheese on top.
  4. Place the second slice of bread on top with the butter side facing up.
  5. Cook over medium to low heat until the bottom is golden brown.
  6. Flip it carefully and cook the other side.
  7. Take it off the pan once the bread is toasted and the cheese is melted.
  8. Let it cool for a minute, then eat it before it gets sad.
