About the Author

Anni Albers (1899–1994) was a German-born textile artist and educator who helped push weaving into the modern art world. She studied at the Bauhaus and became known for treating textiles like design: structure, materials, and visual logic, not just decoration. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

After leaving Germany in 1933, she worked and taught in the United States, including at Black Mountain College. Her book On Weaving (1965) is basically the “serious design brain” version of weaving, and it’s still cited constantly for how it connects craft, industry, and modern design thinking. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
